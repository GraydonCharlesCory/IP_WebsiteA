# Credits for sample media

All videos below come from the Internet Archive (archive.org). Licences are the ones declared on each item's page by its uploader; none is NC or ND. Every file was trimmed and re-encoded to H.264/AAC mp4 (at most 1280x720) with macOS `avconvert` or AVFoundation; nothing else was changed. The Sax-with-band (SAVOY) and Mendelssohn (MENDELSSOHN) sources are 320x240 and contain audio packets that Chrome refuses to decode, so those clips were re-encoded (video and audio) rather than copied. Every other source is 320x240 except KASSEL (640x360); each clip keeps its source resolution. Several clips are different excerpts of the same source video. The performers in these videos are not the fictional demo musicians whose profiles they appear on.
