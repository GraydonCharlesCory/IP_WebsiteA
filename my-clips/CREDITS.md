# Credits for sample media

All videos below come from the Internet Archive (archive.org). Licences are the ones declared on each item's page by its uploader; none is NC or ND. Every file was trimmed and re-encoded (H.264/AAC mp4, at most 960x540) with macOS `avconvert`; nothing else was changed. Several clips are different excerpts of the same source video. The performers in these videos are not the fictional demo musicians whose profiles they appear on.
